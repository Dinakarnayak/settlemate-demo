Private by design. Not by promise.

Visa dates, wages and spending are sensitive. Landed keeps them on your phone, encrypted, and we never see them, because there’s no server to send them to.

Read the privacy policy →

  • 🔐
    AES-256 encryption. The key lives in your phone’s secure Android Keystore.
  • 🚫
    No ads, analytics or trackers. Not now, not later.
  • 👆
    App lock. Fingerprint, face or PIN. Screenshots and the Recents preview are blocked by default.
  • 💾
    Your backup, your key. Passphrase-encrypted backups, and a one-tap “erase everything”.
  • 📍
    Location only when you ask. Rounded to about 1 km for “Food near me”, never stored.

Install in a minute

Landed isn’t on Google Play yet, so you install it directly. It’s free.

Download

On your Android phone, tap Download for Android. You’ll get Landed.apk.

Allow the install

Open the file. If asked, allow your browser to install apps (Settings → Install unknown apps). You can switch it off again afterwards.

Set up

Open Landed, add only what you want to, and turn on App lock in Settings.

Questions

Is Landed really free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases. Landed runs entirely on your phone, so there are no server costs to pass on.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without signing in. Your data stays on your phone either way.

Is this legal or immigration advice?

No. Landed gives general, simplified information and links to official sources such as GOV.UK, UKCISA and the NHS. For your own situation, speak to your university’s international student advice team or a regulated immigration adviser.

What happens if I lose or change my phone?

Create an encrypted backup in Settings and keep it somewhere safe. On your new phone, install Landed and restore it with your passphrase. Without a backup, the data can’t be recovered, which is the point of on-device encryption.

Why isn’t it on Google Play?

Landed is a new, independent student project. Direct download lets it launch for free; a Play Store release may follow.

Is it safe to install an APK?

Only install apps from sources you trust. Landed’s full source code is public on GitHub, and every build is made automatically by GitHub Actions from that code.

Is Landed linked to my university or the Home Office?

No. Landed is independent and not affiliated with UKVI, the Home Office, the NHS or any university.
