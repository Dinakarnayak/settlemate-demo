Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026 · Applies to the Landed Android app

In short: Landed keeps your information on your phone, encrypted. We don’t run ads, use analytics or trackers, or sell data, and we don’t have a server that receives your data.

Who we are

Landed is an independent student project by Dinakar Nayak N, based in Leicester, UK. It isn’t affiliated with UKVI, the Home Office, the NHS or any university. For privacy questions or requests, open an issue on GitHub.

What stays on your phone

Your profile and photo, arrival checklist, work shifts, spending, deadlines, notes and saved guides. They are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and the encryption key is held in your phone’s secure Android Keystore. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this data. We never receive it.

Profile photos are cropped and re-saved by the app, which removes hidden photo data such as GPS location before storage.

The Ask helper runs offline: your questions are not sent anywhere.

Backups

Backups you create are encrypted with a passphrase only you know. You decide where to keep the file. Without the passphrase it can’t be read, including by us.

Permissions

  • Approximate location: only for Food near me, only when you ask.
  • Biometrics: only if you turn on App lock. Your fingerprint or face never leaves your phone’s secure hardware.
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose a profile photo.
  • Internet: to search nearby places, open links and (optionally) sign in.

Referral links

The banks and SIMs section links to providers’ official pages. If a referral link is ever used, it will be clearly labelled “Referral”.

Children

Landed is designed for university students and isn’t aimed at children under 13.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what information is held about you, correct it or delete it. Because your data stays on your phone, you can see, change and erase it in the app yourself (Settings → Erase everything). If you signed in, you can delete your account in Settings → Account. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk.
